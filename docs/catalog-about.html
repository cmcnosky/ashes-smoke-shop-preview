About the catalog

What the labels mean, and how to find your way around.

Selection, with context.

This guide brings together the named brands and products in Ashes’s published store pages, manufacturer retailer lists, shop photographs and dated posts. Each entry carries its source timing and store context.

Named on store pages
The current Ashes website explicitly names the brand or product family. It does not provide live quantities or prices.
Manufacturer listing
A manufacturer’s retailer directory names Ashes. This supports the retailer relationship, not every model made by that brand.
Dated product feature
An Ashes post featured the item on a specific date. A pictured one-off piece may no longer be available.
Undated carry list / shop photo
The name or object appears in a source with no reliable update or capture date.
From the archive / event association
The entry belongs to shop history or an event. It is not presented as current retail stock.

Find and save.

Search by name or product type, combine filters, and open a product or brand for its details. Copy a filtered view’s link to return to the same selection. Saved products stay in this browser; there is no account or order attached.

Prices and purchasing

This is an informational catalog. It does not accept orders or payments, and it does not invent prices, sizes, flavors or stock quantities. Sources were checked November 12, 2026.
