BEFORE YOU VISIT

Common questions

What are your opening hours?+

Denton is open daily, 11am–11pm. Lewisville is open daily, 10am–10pm. Call for holiday or special hours.

How do I check product availability?+

Call Denton at (940) 381-1010 or Lewisville at (469) 968-8008. The product guide shows categories, rather than live stock.

Where can I park?+

Denton has parking out front. Lewisville has nearby street parking and Old Town lots. Choose Denton or Lewisville for directions and parking details.

Can I browse without buying?+

Yes. Take your time and ask questions. Ashes staff do not work on commission.
